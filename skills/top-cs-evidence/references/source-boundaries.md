# Source and online boundaries

Use this source order: author-provided source text or notes; official venue pages for policy; public bibliographic metadata for identity; then an explicit unresolved placeholder.

Online lookup is opt-in and limited to Crossref, arXiv, and DBLP metadata. It may establish a DOI/title/author/year match. It may not download full text, infer novelty, assert claim entailment, or replace an author’s reading of a source.

Keep source excerpts, PDFs, and private notes in the author project. Project metadata stores only IDs, statuses, hashes, and safe relative paths.
